Frame by Frame privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. There's no account, and the app doesn't collect any personal data.

Where your records are kept

Your hives, visits, entries, reminders and photos are kept on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's records (not photos) to your Google account, as it does for other apps.

Backups and exports

When you make a backup or a CSV export, you choose where the file goes. It only leaves your phone if you send or save it somewhere yourself.

Ads and analytics

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Payments

If you buy Pro, Google Play handles the payment. The app only learns whether Pro was bought. It never sees your payment details.

Questions

You can contact the developer through the app's page on Google Play.
